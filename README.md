# Complementary tensor relations and bipartite matching kernels

Preprint by Ignoramus, version 3.3. It differs from version 3.2 only in the wording of the statement on AI use and the author's role; the mathematics is unchanged.

**Version of record:** Zenodo, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.24098044](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24098044). Where that page lists more than one version, the newest is version 3.3. This repository mirrors that record so the source can be browsed and so errors can be reported in the issue tracker.

## What the paper shows

Let D_i be a nonzero tensor occupying every leaf space except V_i, with a common additional factor space X, and consider relations sum_i v_i D_i = 0 with v_i in V_i.

- For n leaves, the relation space has dimension at most n-1 over every field, and equality is characterized by a common factor in X and signed maximal minors of a matrix with separated column variables. This gives sharp bounds for completing any nonempty subset of the last row of a bipartite matching tensor.
- Maximal kernels are classified for two fixed rows and three leaves, over every field and with zero edge blocks allowed, provided the three complementary tensors are nonzero.
- The root-line conclusion gives a sharp cut-rank obstruction on an eight-vertex matching network, over every field of characteristic different from two.

The paper does not claim to settle an open case of the Krenn-Gu conjecture.
